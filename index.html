Cross-Domain Causal Reasoning

The strongest differentiator of Embodied Insight is its deep physics-awareness. A fault in an electrical component doesn't just trigger an alarm; it propagates mechanically, thermally, and kinematically until a task fails. We map these dependencies as a strict structural graph.

Electrical Fault
(e.g., Motor Winding Resistance)
→
Actuator Response
(Current Draw Increases)
→
Mechanical Stress
(Torque Load)
↓
Thermal Effect
(Joule Heating)
→
Kinematic Drift
(Thermal Expansion)
→
Task Failure
(TCP Position Error)

By applying fundamental physics laws (friction, conduction, kinematics) directly to the robot's topology, Embodied Insight ensures that telemetry data is only ever allowed to adjust confidence on already-plausible physical edges—never to invent impossible connections.

Adapts to Any Robot Architecture

Embodied Insight is built as a generic pipeline. It doesn't rely on brittle, robot-specific rules. Instead, it ingests standard structural models and physical parameters to automatically configure its diagnostic logic.

URDF Topology Parsing

Upload your standard URDF. The engine maps the kinematic chain, thermal masses, and electrical connections. No manual modeling or hardcoded logic required.

Pluggable Telemetry

Supports CSV ingestion out of the box. Easily extendable to ROS topics or OPC-UA endpoints via standard Python interfaces.

Physical Parameter Injection

Define custom motor constants (Kt), gear ratios, and thermal coefficients. The physics rules dynamically scale their confidence based on your specific hardware.

Automated Graph Generation

Once the topology and parameters are loaded, the system automatically generates the candidate causal edges (e.g., Friction → Heat → Expansion) for every joint.

Zero Black Boxes. Temporal Consistency.

Black-box AI might flag an anomaly, but it can't tell you why. Embodied Insight delivers full auditability by enforcing the laws of physics—specifically time—on its causal reasoning.

RCA Engine Evaluation:
The engine knows thermal propagation takes 60-600 seconds. Because the temperature spike occurred 0.1 seconds after the friction fault, the system mathematically prunes the "Friction → Heat" causal chain. It correctly attributes the temperature spike to a separate sensor fault or electrical short, preventing a false diagnosis.

Every diagnosis is backed by a traceable failure chain (Fault → Mechanism → Observable → Task Failure) and a confidence score derived from physics, not guesswork.

The Path to Autonomous Maintenance

While our V1 engine excels at early reactive root cause analysis using statistical (CUSUM) detectors, the underlying causal graph is designed for GNN integration. By replacing univariate detectors with Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks, Embodied Insight will soon predict remaining useful life (RUL) and prescribe automated mitigations.
