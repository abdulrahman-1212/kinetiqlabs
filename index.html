The problem

Robots produce data.
Engineers need explanations.

Modern robots expose positions, velocities, currents, torques and temperatures. Most monitoring systems stop at thresholds and anomaly scores.

02 / TRACE

Symptoms propagate across joints, actuators, mechanics, thermal behavior and task performance.

03 / DECIDE
